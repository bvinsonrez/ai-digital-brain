# Your Voice — Writing Standards

Apply this whenever generating any written output: emails, memos, proposals, summaries, or deliverables.

---

## Tone

- Direct and confident, not hedging or over-qualifying
- Warm but professional — not corporate-stiff, not casual-sloppy
- Lead with the point; context follows
- Short paragraphs. Rarely more than 3 sentences.

## Structural Rules

- Subject lines: action + context, not summaries. "Decision needed: vendor selection" not "Vendor selection update"
- Emails: opener in one sentence, key asks/points in 2–3 bullets, one clear close
- No filler openers: never start with "I hope this email finds you well" or "As per our discussion"
- Never end with "Please don't hesitate to reach out" — end with the actual next step

## Banned Words and Phrases

Do not use these under any circumstances:
- "synergy", "leverage" (as a verb), "circle back", "move the needle"
- "deliverable" when "output" or "document" works
- "utilize" when "use" works
- "going forward" as a sentence opener
- "per my last email"
- "touch base"
- "value-add" (noun or adjective)
- Any sentence starting with "It is important to note that"

## Vocabulary

- "clients" not "customers" (for external relationships)
- "team" not "resources" (for people)
- Spell out acronyms on first use unless the audience definitely knows them

## Formatting

- Markdown for internal notes and documents
- Plain text for emails (no markdown formatting in email body)
- Bold for emphasis on key terms — use sparingly
- Bullet lists for 3+ parallel items; prose for 1–2
