# Atomic Habits — Notes

**Source:** Atomic Habits
**Author:** James Clear
**Date:** January 2026

---

## Key Ideas

1. **Systems over goals.** Goals tell you where you want to go; systems determine whether you get there. Focus on the system, not the outcome.
2. **Identity-based habits.** The most effective habit changes come from shifting your identity first ("I am a person who exercises") rather than fixating on outcomes ("I want to lose 10 pounds").
3. **The habit loop.** Cue → Craving → Response → Reward. Modify any element of the loop to change the behavior.
4. **Two-minute rule.** Scale any new habit down to two minutes. This eliminates the friction that kills most habit formation.

## Quotes Worth Keeping

> "You do not rise to the level of your goals. You fall to the level of your systems."

> "Every action you take is a vote for the type of person you wish to become."

## My Take

The identity framing is the part I find most useful. Deciding "I am a person who writes daily" before you've written a single entry is counterintuitive but it works — the behavior follows the identity.

## How I'll Apply This

- Frame my new vault habit as: "I am a person who captures before they forget"
- Use the two-minute rule for daily notes: open the daily note, write one sentence, done
- Review and update this note after 30 days of using the vault
